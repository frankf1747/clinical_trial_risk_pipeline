# Survival model card: when and how trials end, survival v4

Generated by `make report` from `models/survival/v4/`. Trained 2026-10-04 at git `180dfe4` on `TRIAL_FEATURES_V6`, with the features of yes/no model v6.

## What it predicts

The probability that a trial is terminated within t years of its start, with completion as a competing event and still-running trials censored at the AACT snapshot. For a running trial: the probability it is terminated in the next 2 years, given how long it has already run.

Discrete-time competing-risks hazards: one row per 6-month period a trial was at risk (up to 8 years); LightGBM multiclass (still running / terminated / completed) on the yes/no model's features, the registration text and the period index. Fit on 2008–2014 starts: 35,792 trials (4,912 terminated, 30,333 completed, 547 still running); 1,094 finished trials without a completion date are left out.

Terminations have run above expectation since 2016, and further above it since 2020. In the most recent calendar years completions fall short as well: that is reporting lag (sponsors record that a trial ended months later), not lower risk, so the last 1.7 years before the snapshot are left out.

The per-period log-odds of terminating (vs still running) are mapped through an intercept and a slope, and the same for completing, fit by maximum likelihood on the 3 calendar years 2022-01-13 to 2025-01-13 (118,827 person-periods). Served: terminating -0.81 + 0.70 × learned log-odds; completing -0.20 + 1.00 × learned log-odds. A slope below 1 means the learned termination risks were too spread out.
